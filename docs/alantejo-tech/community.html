Community

A project from 351 Startups + Start Campus.

Start Campus is building Europe's largest sustainable data-centre campus — 1.2 GW of capacity, six buildings, €8.5B invested, Microsoft and Nscale already deployed. 351 Startups is Portugal's largest startup community — 1,500+ founders, programmes nationwide, no central campus. Alentejo Tech is the bridge: Start Campus's infrastructure + 351's people + the Alentejo's land.

Start Campus

The infrastructure.

SINES DC is targeting 1.2 GW of IT capacity across six buildings (SIN01–SIN06), powered 100% by renewables at PUE 1.1. SIN01 is operational; SIN02 ramps in 2026; SIN03 onwards through 2030. Microsoft's $10B AI build-out with Nscale is already running on 12,600+ NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs at SIN01 — scaling to 66,000+ NVIDIA Rubin GPUs from late 2027.

351 Startups

The people.

Portugal's largest startup association: 1,500+ founders on Slack, programmes including Techstars Community Leader Academy, Startup Weekend Portugal and Portugal Tech Week (100+ events across 9 cities), Community Funds, and €700k+ in software perks. Now anchoring an Alentejo chapter on the Sines platform.

Why a tech region

Anchors aren't an ecosystem. Yet.

A 1.2 GW data centre creates jobs. A €2.8B green-hydrogen project creates jobs. A 130,000-ha irrigated agritech belt creates jobs. None of them, alone, creates a startup ecosystem. Alentejo Tech is the connective tissue: from Aernnova's aerostructures floor to PACT's aerospace founders, from EDIA's irrigation grid to CEBAL's biotech lab, from Start Campus's GPU racks to the AI builders renting them. One region — one programme grid, one founder community, one funnel.
