9 Capital Modernization Reforms

01 Closest to Execution

Launch Fund-of-Funds

A Portugal Tech Fund II — investor-friendly, with minimal reporting strings — is the fastest way to channel existing domestic wealth into venture.

Owner: Banco de Fomento / EIF
02

Angel Co-Investment Matching

Fast, simplified co-investment matching for specific tickets up to €250K. No long pre-assessment. No complex reporting that slows round closing.

Owner: Portugal Ventures
03

Angel Tax Relief & Capital Recycling

Annual deduction for invested amounts, exit incentives to recycle gains into new startups. The goal is capital recycling — not tax subsidy.

Owner: Ministry of Finance
04

Unlock Institutional LP Participation

Lowering regulatory barriers for pension funds and insurers to allocate 2% to local venture via specialist vehicles and targeted incentives.

Owner: ASF / Ministry of Finance
05

Startup Fund Light

Funds under €500M should follow lighter EU-style regulatory treatment. Speed and simplicity attract domestic capital into venture products.

Owner: CMVM
06

SIFIDE Relaunch 2.0

Broaden eligible expenses beyond R&D to growth activities. Require 30-50% private capital inside each fund as a market-discipline filter.

Owner: ANI / Ministry of Economy
07

CMVM + EU Inc Harmonization

Simplify regulation to best-in-class EU standards. Add EU Inc notary compatibility, startup-specialized judicial routes, and easier M&A processes.

Owner: CMVM / Ministry of Justice
08

Create Incentives to Boost M&A

Broad tax structures that increase strategic acquisitions and liquidity events. M&A is a capital recycling engine that returns wealth to founders.

Owner: Ministry of Finance
09

Long-Term Cross-Party Startup Capital Consensus

Tactic harmonization across political cycles. Stability is a prerequisite for mobilizing patient domestic capital through a non-partisan framework.

Owner: Government + Cross-Party
